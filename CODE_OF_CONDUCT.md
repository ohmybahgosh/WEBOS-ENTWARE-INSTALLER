# Code of conduct

This project is a small, friendly corner of the webOS homebrew world.  Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity, and whether they're filing their first bug report or sending a pull request.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.**  Plenty of people here are rooting a TV for the first time.  Explain things without talking down.
- **Assume good intent.**  A short message is usually a busy person, not a rude one.
- **Keep it about the work.**  Disagree with ideas and code, never with the person behind them.
- **Help people help you.**  Share what you ran, your TV model and the `doctor` output, and leave out passwords, home IP addresses and anything else private.
- **Respect people's time and choices.**  A "no" to a feature, or a closed issue, isn't personal.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, insults, threats or slurs, in public or in private messages.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Discrimination or personal attacks based on who someone is.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, self-promotion that isn't about the project, and trolling.
- Encouraging anyone to harm their TV or someone else's, or to get around the law.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project lives: issues, pull requests, discussions, commits and code review, and anywhere else someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, tell the maintainer privately through the [contact form](https://ohmybahgosh.dev/contact/?t=mail-private).  Reports are kept confidential.  You'll get a reply, and nobody is punished for reporting in good faith.

## What happens next

The maintainer reviews every report and decides what's fair, which might be a quiet word, a removed comment, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project.  Anyone asked to stop is expected to stop right away.

This code of conduct takes inspiration from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
